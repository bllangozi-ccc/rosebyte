# Rights and licence status

This is a research preview with public documentation, specifications and examples, plus owner-encrypted core releases. No open-source or redistribution licence is granted at this stage. Rights holders retain their rights. Hosted access, encryption and software licensing are separate matters. Previously downloaded plaintext cannot be technically revoked remotely.

Third-party components retain their own licences. The qualified native runtime includes json-c under its Expat/MIT terms, with its original notices in the authorised release. Project restrictions do not replace those third-party terms.
